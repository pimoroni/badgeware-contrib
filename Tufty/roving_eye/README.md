# Roving Eye

A watchful eye that darts about, scans the room, gets suspicious, blinks, and
every now and then stares straight at whoever is reading your badge. It wakes
up from a nap each time it starts.

![The roving eye in action](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jeffred13/Tufty2350-Roving-Eye/main/images/preview.gif)

## Buttons

- **A:** glance left
- **B:** stare at the viewer (or jump in surprise)
- **C:** glance right

## Light reactions (optional)

With a [Multi-Sensor Stick](https://shop.pimoroni.com/products/multi-sensor-stick?variant=42169525633107)
plugged in, the eye reacts to the light: the pupil shrinks in bright light and
widens in the dark, a sudden flash makes it squint and blink, and if the lights
stay off it dozes off until they come back on. Without the stick it simply
skips this.
